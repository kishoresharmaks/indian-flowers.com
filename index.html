Commercial licensing

Software you can sell with clarity.

A Nexus client license grants a limited right to run one named business on one approved deployment. It does not transfer the codebase, resale rights or intellectual property.

  • Unique license key and deployment record per client
  • Domain and installation binding
  • Private source repository and protected infrastructure secrets
  • Written EULA or commercial software agreement for every client
