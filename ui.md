# Indian-Flowers.com — UI Design System & Typography Specification

## 1. Visual Brand Concept: "The Flower House"

A contemporary floral atelier with a botanical-green identity, soft warm ivory canvas, restrained rose accents, generous white space, and rich natural flower photography.

## 3. Typography Usage Rules

1. **Font Roles**:
   - `DM Serif Display`: Reserved for editorial headlines, hero banners, section titles, and promotional occasion banners.
   - `DM Sans`: Used across all commerce UI (product names, prices, navigation items, buttons, form controls, body copy, and metadata).
2. **Text Hierarchy**:
   - Never use all-caps for product names, buttons, or multi-word labels.
   - Eyebrows and status badges may use uppercase with letter-spacing `0.08em` to `0.18em`.
   - Prices always use US Dollars (`$`) with tabular numerals.
3. **Line Length & Contrast**:
   - Keep heading line lengths constrained to avoid single-word wrap orphans.
   - Body copy should default to 14–16px with line height of 1.5–1.65.
